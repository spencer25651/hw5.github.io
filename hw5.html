Desk Lamp: Hierarchical Model with Multiple Lights

Edit the shaders on the left; the canvas updates as you type.
Drag with the mouse to rotate the model.
Use the arrow keys and w, s keys to move the camera.
Use the slider and dropdown to rotate about an axis; the button starts/stops spinning.
The panels on the right pose the lamp, move objects, and edit the lights and materials. The lamp's spotlight is attached to its head, so it follows the hierarchy.
